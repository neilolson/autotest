# Rainbow Beat Ranch Guardians

A kid-friendly game concept that combines:
- **Rhythm dance battles** (from idea #1), and
- **Creative unicorn-and-rabbit ranch building** (from idea #2).

The player builds a magical concert ranch, performs songs, defeats gloom with dance, and unlocks everything through play.

## 1) Core Fantasy
You are the leader of a glittery unicorn idol team with rabbit backup dancers. During the day, you build a rainbow ranch music park. At showtime, you dance to restore music and light stolen by “Shadow Noise” and “Gloom Gremlins.”

## 2) Main Gameplay Loop (for age 5)
1. **Build Mode (5–10 min)**
   - Place simple objects: stages, speakers, rainbow arches, bunny houses, glitter trees.
   - One-tap placement and rotate buttons only (no complicated menus).
2. **Show Mode (1–2 min songs)**
   - Inputs: tap, hold, swipe.
   - Accuracy creates “Rainbow Power” that clears darkness and boosts crowd joy.
3. **Reward Mode (30–60 sec)**
   - Open reward bubbles: stars, stickers, decor, costumes.
   - Optional “decorate now” prompt to return directly to Build Mode.

## 3) Mode Breakdown
### Build Mode
- Freely place decor in a safe sandbox.
- “Auto-pretty” button aligns and beautifies the area in one click.
- Rabbits wander, dance, and react to placed items.

### Show Mode
- Short tracks with 3 difficulty lanes: **Tiny Beats**, **Happy Beats**, **Super Star**.
- No fail state in Tiny Beats.
- “Boss show” every few songs where synced dance purifies a big gloom cloud into confetti.

### Festival Mode (weekly in-game event)
- Theme examples: **Cotton Candy Night**, **Neon Carrot Party**, **Moonlight Remix**.
- Complete 3 easy challenges to earn an exclusive song remix and matching decor set.

## 4) Progression & Unlocks (No Payments Ever)
### Currencies (earned only)
- **Star Notes**: earned from songs; used for decor and costumes.
- **Friend Hearts**: earned by helping rabbits; used for companion unlocks.
- **Rainbow Keys**: earned from milestones; used for major stage upgrades.

### Unlock Paths
- **Songs**: clear story shows + festivals.
- **Decor Packs**: spend Star Notes + complete style quests.
- **Unicorn/Rabbit Cosmetics**: friendship milestones + sticker album goals.
- **Dance Emotes & VFX**: combo streak achievements.
- **New Rabbit Helpers**: side quests (“Find Lost Drumsticks,” “Light the Disco Carrots”).

## 5) Content Plan (starter scope)
### Worlds
1. **Cloud Meadow Stage** (onboarding)
2. **Neon Carrot Disco** (rabbit-focused festival area)
3. **Moonlight Idol Arena** (grand finales + remix tracks)

### Starter Content Targets
- 12 songs (8 base + 4 remix unlocks)
- 60 decor items
- 24 costumes (12 unicorn, 12 rabbit)
- 18 sticker achievements
- 6 rabbit companions

## 6) Accessibility & Parent Features
- Large UI buttons and icon-first navigation.
- Optional **Chill Mode**: no fail states, auto-timing assist, slower note speed.
- Optional narration for non-readers.
- Playtime reminder options (15/30/45 minutes).
- Fully offline capable and ad-free.

## 7) Safety / Tone Rules
- No horror visuals.
- Villains are abstract “noise clouds,” never violent enemies.
- “Winning” always framed as restoring joy/music, not hurting others.
- Positive celebration after every session.

## 8) First Play Session Example (10 minutes)
1. Meet Sparkle the Unicorn + Bop the Rabbit.
2. Place first stage and rainbow arch.
3. Play Song 1 (“Shine Bounce”).
4. Earn first reward bubble (new bunny house).
5. Place reward item and trigger a mini dance party.
6. End with sticker unlock + “See you at next show!”

## 9) Non-Negotiable Monetization Rule
- **No microtransactions.**
- **No paid currency.**
- **No premium pass.**
- **All unlockables must be earned through gameplay only.**

## 10) MVP Build Plan (first playable)
### Goal
Ship a polished 15–20 minute vertical slice proving the combined loop is fun.

### MVP Includes
- 1 world: **Cloud Meadow Stage**.
- 3 songs (Tiny Beats + Happy Beats).
- 20 decor items.
- 6 costumes.
- 2 rabbit helpers.
- 1 mini boss show with confetti purification finale.

### MVP Success Criteria
- A 5-year-old can complete 3 sessions with minimal help.
- Session time naturally loops between building and rhythm play.
- Players unlock at least 1 meaningful reward every session.

## 11) Reward Cadence (designed for young attention spans)
- **Every song:** small reward bubble (coins/stickers/decor piece).
- **Every 3 songs:** medium reward (costume or emote).
- **Every 10 songs or event clear:** major reward (new helper rabbit, stage skin, or remix).
- **Daily first login:** free cheerful bonus pack (non-random and kid-safe).

## 12) UI/UX Notes for Prototype
- Home screen has 3 giant buttons only: **Build**, **Dance**, **Rewards**.
- Always show “what to do next” card with one action (e.g., “Play 1 song to unlock carrot lights”).
- Use pictograms + voice lines instead of text-heavy instructions.
- Keep camera controls minimal: pan + reset camera button.

## 13) Audio & Music Direction
- Songs should be upbeat, playful, and short, with clear beat accents.
- Include call-and-response vocal cues (“Tap now!”, “Great sparkle!”).
- Offer “Soft Sound” toggle for sensitive listeners.
- Add celebratory stingers for every unlock to reinforce joy and progress.

## 14) Future Expansion (after MVP)
- Co-op parent-child dance mode.
- “Design Challenge” sharing via local save codes (no open chat).
- Seasonal festivals with fully earnable themed tracks and decor.
- Story chapters featuring new rabbit crews and unicorn idol rivals.
